ZYRA LUXE - QUERY ROUTER WORKFLOW
============================================================

User Query
     ↓

     ↓
Check Intent
     ↓
┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
↓          ↓          ↓          ↓
Product   Policy     Order     General
↓          ↓          ↓          ↓
Product   Pinecone   Live API   General

Examples
--------

"Show me earrings"
        ↓
Product

"Can I return this?"
        ↓
Policy

"Where is my order?"
        ↓
Order

"Hello"
        ↓
General


Important
---------

Router decides which system
should handle the query.

It does not answer the query.
