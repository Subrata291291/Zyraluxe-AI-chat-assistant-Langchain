============================================================
FILE 56 - SEARCH + RANKING WORKFLOW
============================================================

Purpose:
Combine product search and product ranking.

Input:
Customer search filters.

Process:

Customer Filters
      ↓
ProductSearch
      ↓
Matching Products
      ↓
ProductRanking
      ↓
Ranked Products
      ↓
Top Products

Example:

Category: earrings
Maximum Price: ₹300
Stock: True

Output:
Best matching products first.
