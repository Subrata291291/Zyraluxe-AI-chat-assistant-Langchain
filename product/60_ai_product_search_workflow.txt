============================================================
FILE 60 - AI PRODUCT SEARCH WORKFLOW
============================================================

Purpose:
Provide an end-to-end AI product search flow.

Input:
Natural language customer request.

Process:

Customer Query
      ↓
LLM Query Parser
      ↓
Structured Filters
      ↓
Product Search
      ↓
Product Ranking
      ↓
Ranked Products
      ↓
Customer Response

Example:

Customer:
"I need oxidized earrings under 300 that are available."

Filters:

{
    "query": "oxidized earrings",
    "category": "earrings",
    "max_price": 300,
    "in_stock": true
}

Output:
Ranked matching products.
