============================================================
ZYRA LUXE - GENERAL RESPONSE WORKFLOW
============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
Generate natural conversational responses for GENERAL queries.

FLOW
----
User message
    ↓
GeneralResponseGenerator
    ↓
Conversation history
    ↓
Prompt creation
    ↓
ProductionLLMManager
    ↓
Gemini
    ↓ failure
Groq
    ↓ failure
OpenRouter
    ↓ failure
OpenAI
    ↓
Natural response


IMPORTANT
---------
This module does NOT handle:

- Product search
- Product price
- Product stock
- Order status
- Return policy
- Refund policy

Those are handled by dedicated modules.
