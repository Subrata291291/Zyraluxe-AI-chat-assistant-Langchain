============================================================
FILE 61 - PRODUCT EMBEDDING PREPARATION WORKFLOW
============================================================

Purpose:
Prepare structured product data for semantic search.

Input:
data/products/products.json

Process:

Product JSON
      ↓
Select searchable fields
      ↓
Combine product information
      ↓
Create searchable text
      ↓
Prepare embedding records

Searchable fields:

- Product name
- Categories
- Tags
- Description

Do NOT use price or stock as semantic content.

Output:
data/products/product_embedding_records.json
