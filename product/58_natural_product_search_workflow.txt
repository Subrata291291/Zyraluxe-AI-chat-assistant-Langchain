============================================================
FILE 58 - NATURAL PRODUCT SEARCH WORKFLOW
============================================================

Purpose:
Convert a natural language customer request into product
filters and return ranked products.

Input:
Natural language customer query.

Example:
"Show me earrings under ₹300 that are available."

Process:

Customer Query
      ↓
57 - Product Query Parser
      ↓
Structured Filters
      ↓
56 - Search + Ranking
      ↓
Ranked Products
      ↓
Customer Results

Example Filters:

{
    "category": "earrings",
    "max_price": 300,
    "in_stock": true
}
