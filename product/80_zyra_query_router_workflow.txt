==================================================
FILE 80 - ZYRA LUXE QUERY ROUTER
==================================================

Goal:
Identify the type of customer query.

Routes:

PRODUCT
POLICY
ORDER
GENERAL

Process:

Customer Query
      ↓
Normalize Query
      ↓
Check Order Keywords
      ↓
Check Policy Keywords
      ↓
Check Product Keywords
      ↓
Otherwise General
      ↓
Return Route


Examples:

"I want oxidized jewellery"
        ↓
PRODUCT

"Show me earrings under 300"
        ↓
PRODUCT

"What is your return policy?"
        ↓
POLICY

"Can I get a refund?"
        ↓
POLICY

"Where is my order?"
        ↓
ORDER

"Hello"
        ↓
GENERAL


Purpose:

The router decides which backend
should handle the customer query.
