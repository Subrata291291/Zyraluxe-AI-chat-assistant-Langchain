============================================================
FILE 57 - PRODUCT QUERY PARSER WORKFLOW
============================================================

Purpose:
Convert natural language product requests into structured
product filters.

Input:
Natural language customer query.

Example:
"Show me earrings under ₹300 that are available."

Output:

{
    "category": "earrings",
    "max_price": 300,
    "in_stock": true
}

Flow:

Customer Query
      ↓
Keyword Detection
      ↓
Price Extraction
      ↓
Stock Detection
      ↓
Structured Filters
      ↓
Search + Ranking
