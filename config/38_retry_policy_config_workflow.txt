============================================================
ZYRA LUXE - RETRY POLICY CONFIGURATION
============================================================

18_config.py
     ↓
MAX_LLM_RETRIES
RETRY_BASE_DELAY
     ↓
Retry Policy
     ↓
LLM Manager


Example
-------

MAX_LLM_RETRIES = 2
RETRY_BASE_DELAY = 2

Retry 1 → 2 seconds
Retry 2 → 4 seconds


Purpose
-------

Keep retry settings centralized.

Changing retry behavior
does not require changing
the LLM Manager code.
