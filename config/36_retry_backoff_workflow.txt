============================================================
ZYRA LUXE - RETRY + EXPONENTIAL BACKOFF
============================================================

LLM/API Operation
       ↓
     Failed
       ↓
    Retry #1
       ↓
    Wait 2 sec
       ↓
     Failed
       ↓
    Retry #2
       ↓
    Wait 4 sec
       ↓
    Success
       ↓
    Continue


Delay Formula
-------------

delay = base_delay × 2^attempt

Attempt 1 → 2 seconds
Attempt 2 → 4 seconds
Attempt 3 → 8 seconds


Purpose
-------

Handle temporary failures before
switching to another LLM provider.
